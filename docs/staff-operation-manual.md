## Getting Started

### What is the Okaeri Resort System?

The Okaeri Resort System is your comprehensive digital tool for managing all
internal resort operations. This system is designed exclusively for **staff
use** and helps you:

- Track bookings and guest information
- Manage expenses and financial records
- Generate reports for business operations
- Monitor resort performance metrics
- Handle daily operational tasks

### Who Can Use This System?

- ✅ **Resort Staff Members** (Front desk, housekeeping, management)
- ✅ **Authorized Personnel** with login credentials
- ❌ **Guests/Customers** (This is internal-only)

### Device Requirements

**Recommended Devices**:

- 📱 **Mobile Phones**: iPhone or Android (primary)
- 💻 **Tablets**: iPad or Android tablets
- 🖥️ **Computers**: Any modern browser

**Supported Browsers**:

- ✅ **Chrome** (Recommended)
- ✅ **Firefox**
- ✅ **Safari** (iOS/Mac)
- ✅ **Edge**

## Accessing the System

### Step 1: Get Your Login Credentials

Your supervisor will provide you with:

- 📧 **Email address**: Your work email
- 🔐 **Temporary password**: Change this on first login
- 🏢 **Project access**: Okaeri Resort project

### Step 2: First-Time Login

1. **Open your browser** and go to the system URL
2. **Enter your email** in the login form
3. **Enter your temporary password**
4. **Click "Login"**
5. **Follow the password change prompts**

### Step 3: Mobile App Installation (Recommended)

**For Android phones**:

1. Open Chrome browser
2. Visit the system URL
3. Look for "Add to Home Screen" popup
4. Tap "Add" to install the app

**For iPhones**:

1. Open Safari browser
2. Visit the system URL
3. Tap the Share button (box with arrow)
4. Select "Add to Home Screen"
5. Tap "Add" to confirm

### Password Requirements

Your new password must have:

- ✅ At least 8 characters
- ✅ At least one uppercase letter
- ✅ At least one lowercase letter
- ✅ At least one number
- ✅ At least one special character (@, #, $, etc.)

## Dashboard Overview

When you log in, you'll see your **Financial Dashboard** - your main control
center.

### Understanding Your Dashboard

#### Key Metrics Cards (Top Row)

1. **📊 Total Revenue**
   - Shows current period income
   - Includes all booking payments
   - Updates in real-time

2. **💰 Total Expenses**
   - Displays period spending
   - All recorded expenses
   - Budget tracking included

3. **🏨 Occupancy Rate**
   - Current room utilization
   - Percentage of occupied rooms
   - Historical comparison

4. **📈 Net Profit**
   - Revenue minus expenses
   - Period performance indicator
   - Trend comparison

#### Quick Actions (Top Right)

- **🔄 Refresh**: Updates all data
- **📅 Period Selector**: Choose time range (Daily, Weekly, Monthly)
- **📁 Export**: Download reports (Excel format)

#### Charts and Reports (Main Area)

- **Revenue Trends**: Line chart showing income over time
- **Expense Breakdown**: Pie chart of spending categories
- **Payment Methods**: Chart showing payment types
- **Cash Flow**: Bar chart of money in/out

### Navigation Menu

**Main Sections**:

- 🏠 **Dashboard**: Your current view
- 📋 **Bookings**: Reservation management
- 💳 **Expenses**: Financial tracking
- 📊 **Reports**: Detailed analytics
- ⚙️ **Settings**: Account preferences
- 🚪 **Logout**: End your session

## Booking Management

### Creating a New Booking

#### Step 1: Access Booking Form

1. Click **"Bookings"** in the menu
2. Click **"New Booking"** button
3. Fill in guest information

#### Step 2: Guest Information

- **👤 Guest Name**: Full name as on ID
- **📞 Phone Number**: Primary contact
- **📧 Email**: For confirmations (optional)
- **🆔 ID Number**: Government ID reference

#### Step 3: Booking Details

- **📅 Check-in Date**: Arrival date
- **📅 Check-out Date**: Departure date
- **🏠 Room Type**: Select available room
- **👥 Number of Guests**: Total occupants

#### Step 4: Payment Information

- **💰 Total Amount**: Auto-calculated
- **💳 Payment Method**:
  - Cash
  - Bank Transfer
  - GCash
  - Credit/Debit Card
- **🧾 Receipt Number**: If applicable

#### Step 5: Save Booking

1. **Review all information**
2. **Click "Create Booking"**
3. **Print confirmation** if needed

### Managing Existing Bookings

#### Finding Bookings

- **🔍 Search by**: Guest name, phone, or booking ID
- **📅 Filter by**: Date range or status
- **📋 Sort by**: Date, amount, or status

#### Booking Status Options

- **✅ Confirmed**: Booking is valid
- **⏳ Pending**: Awaiting confirmation
- **❌ Cancelled**: Booking cancelled
- **✅ Completed**: Guest checked out

#### Updating Bookings

1. **Find the booking** using search
2. **Click on booking** to open details
3. **Click "Edit"** button
4. **Make changes** as needed
5. **Save changes**

## Financial Operations

### Recording Expenses

#### Why Record Expenses?

- Track resort spending
- Monitor budget compliance
- Generate financial reports
- Maintain business records

#### Step 1: Create New Expense

1. Go to **"Expenses"** section
2. Click **"New Expense"** button
3. Fill in expense details

#### Step 2: Expense Categories

Choose the appropriate category:

- **🏠 Maintenance**: Repairs, cleaning supplies
- **🍴 Food & Beverage**: Kitchen supplies, guest amenities
- **⚡ Utilities**: Electricity, water, internet
- **📢 Marketing**: Advertising, promotional materials
- **👥 Staff**: Salaries, benefits (if authorized)
- **🏢 Administrative**: Office supplies, legal fees
- **🚗 Transportation**: Vehicle fuel, maintenance
- **🎯 Other**: Miscellaneous expenses

#### Step 3: Receipt Upload

- **📷 Take photo** of receipt with phone
- **📁 Upload file** if already digital
- **📝 Add description** of what was purchased
- **💰 Enter amount** (must match receipt)

#### Step 4: Vendor Information

- **🏪 Vendor Name**: Where you bought items
- **📋 TIN Number**: For business receipts (optional)
- **🧾 Receipt Number**: From the receipt
- **📅 Date**: When purchase was made

### Generating Reports

#### Monthly Financial Summary

1. Go to **"Reports"** section
2. Select **"Monthly Summary"**
3. Choose month and year
4. Click **"Generate Report"**
5. **Download** or **Print** as needed

#### Expense Reports

- **By Category**: See spending by type
- **By Vendor**: Track supplier spending
- **By Date Range**: Custom period reports
- **Tax Reports**: For accounting (if needed)

#### Revenue Reports

- **Booking Revenue**: All guest payments
- **Payment Methods**: Cash vs digital payments
- **Occupancy Analysis**: Room utilization
- **Trend Analysis**: Month-over-month comparison

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Can't Log In

**Problem**: "Invalid email or password" **Solutions**:

1. ✅ Check email spelling carefully
2. ✅ Ensure caps lock is off
3. ✅ Try password reset if needed
4. ✅ Contact supervisor for help

**Problem**: "Account is disabled" **Solution**: Contact your supervisor
immediately

#### App Running Slowly

**Solutions**:

1. ✅ **Close other apps** on your phone
2. ✅ **Restart the app** completely
3. ✅ **Check internet connection**
4. ✅ **Clear browser cache** if using browser
5. ✅ **Restart device** if problems persist

#### Can't Upload Photos

**Solutions**:

1. ✅ **Check camera permissions**
2. ✅ **Ensure file size** is under 10MB
3. ✅ **Try different photo format**
4. ✅ **Compress image** if too large

#### Data Not Syncing

**Indicators**:

- 🔴 Red sync icon: Not synced
- 🟡 Yellow sync icon: Syncing in progress
- 🟢 Green sync icon: Successfully synced

**Solutions**:

1. ✅ **Check internet connection**
2. ✅ **Wait for automatic sync**
3. ✅ **Pull down to refresh**
4. ✅ **Restart app** if needed

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Is my data safe in the system?** A: Yes, all data is encrypted and securely
stored. Only authorized staff can access it.

**Q: Can I access the system from home?** A: This is an internal system. Access
policies are set by management.

**Q: What if I forget my password?** A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the
login page or contact your supervisor.

**Q: Can I use the system on multiple devices?** A: Yes, but only one active
session at a time for security.

### Booking Questions

**Q: Can I modify a booking after check-in?** A: Contact management for check-in
date changes. Other details can be updated.

**Q: What if a guest doesn't show up?** A: Mark booking as "No Show" and follow
resort policy for payment.

**Q: How do I handle group bookings?** A: Create individual bookings for each
room, noting the group connection.

### Financial Questions

**Q: What receipts do I need to keep?** A: All receipts for expenses should be
photographed and uploaded immediately.

**Q: Can I record personal expenses?** A: No, only resort business expenses
should be recorded.

**Q: What if I made a mistake in an expense?** A: Contact your supervisor to
help correct the entry.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why is the app asking for camera permission?** A: Camera access is needed
to photograph receipts and documents.

**Q: Does the app use a lot of data?** A: No, the app is optimized for minimal
data usage. Photos use the most data.

**Q: Can I use the system without internet?** A: Yes, basic functions work
offline. Data syncs when internet returns.

## System Tips for Success

### Daily Best Practices

#### Start of Day

1. ✅ **Open the app** and check for overnight updates
2. ✅ **Review dashboard** for key metrics
3. ✅ **Check pending bookings** for today
4. ✅ **Sync any offline data**

#### During Operations

1. ✅ **Enter data immediately** when possible
2. ✅ **Take photos** of receipts right away
3. ✅ **Update booking statuses** as changes occur
4. ✅ **Use search function** to find information quickly

#### End of Day

1. ✅ **Complete any pending entries**
2. ✅ **Upload remaining receipts**
3. ✅ **Sync all data**
4. ✅ **Log out properly**

### Efficiency Tips

#### Keyboard Shortcuts (Desktop)

- **Ctrl+N**: New booking
- **Ctrl+F**: Search
- **Ctrl+R**: Refresh
- **Ctrl+P**: Print current view

#### Mobile Gestures

- **Pull down**: Refresh data
- **Swipe left**: Quick actions
- **Long press**: Additional options
- **Pinch to zoom**: Enlarge text/images

### Data Quality Tips

#### Accurate Information

- ✅ **Double-check spellings** especially names
- ✅ **Verify phone numbers** before saving
- ✅ **Confirm amounts** match receipts exactly
- ✅ **Use proper categories** for expenses

#### Consistent Formatting

- **Dates**: Use the date picker when available
- **Phone numbers**: Include country code if international
- **Amounts**: Don't include currency symbols (₱ is automatic)
- **Names**: Use proper capitalization
