# Booking status changes are enforced in the database, with a short undo for the person who acted

Staff check guests in and out from a phone, where a wrong tap is easy, so two backward moves exist: CheckedIn back to Confirmed and CheckedOut back to CheckedIn. Any role may undo their own check-in or check-out within 2 minutes; after that, only a manager or above may revert. Because a revert is a manager power, the allowed moves and their role rules live in the database, not only in the client, so no one can bypass them through the API. The bookings table stores who made the last status change and when, so the database can judge the 2-minute window. We rejected reading the audit log for this, because a status rule would then depend on the audit system. We rejected undo for managers only, because staff at the cabins are the ones who mis-tap. Restoring a cancelled booking and un-confirming a booking are not allowed: both involve payments and guest messages, so staff enter a new booking instead.

Check-in is allowed only from the check-in day, so only a manager or above may change the dates of a Confirmed booking. Otherwise staff could move the check-in day to today and then check the guest in early. An early arrival is a manager's call.

Which parts of a booking may be edited also depends on its status, and the database enforces this for the same reason. Pending and Confirmed bookings are fully editable, apart from the date rule above. A CheckedIn booking keeps its check-in date; only a manager or above may change its stay, to extend it or end it early. A CheckedOut booking allows only guest details and notes, and its total stays a manager's call. A Cancelled booking allows only its internal note. An anonymized booking allows nothing.
