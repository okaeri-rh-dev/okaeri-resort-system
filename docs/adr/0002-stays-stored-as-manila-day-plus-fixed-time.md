# Stays are stored as a Manila day plus a fixed check-in/check-out time

A booking's check-in and check-out stay in the existing TIMESTAMPTZ columns as the Manila check-in day at the check-in time and the Manila check-out day at the check-out time (`RESORT_OPERATION_HOURS`, 14:00 and 12:00). The app passes Manila days (`YYYY-MM-DD`) everywhere, and one function in `src/lib/bookings/` turns them into instants. We chose this over migrating to DATE columns because the overlap guard (`tstzrange '[)'` exclusion on `booking_unit_availability`), three SQL functions and every reader already work on instants, and a check-out time before the check-in time keeps same-day turnover free. Both times stay at 08:00 Manila or later so a UTC `::date` cast still gives the Manila day. Before this, forms stored three conventions (15:00/11:00 Manila, browser-local midnight, the current time of day), which caused false conflicts and one-day shifts in UTC readers and pricing.
