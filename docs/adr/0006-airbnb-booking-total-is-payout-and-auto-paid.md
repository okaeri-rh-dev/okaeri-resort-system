# An Airbnb booking's total is the payout, and it is paid on entry

Airbnb bookings have no integration: staff enter them by hand with booking source Airbnb. Their total is the payout the resort receives, typed by staff, not the guest's Airbnb price and not the resort's pricing engine; on a multi-cabin booking it is split across cabins in proportion to the engine's price. When staff enter the booking, the app also records one payment of the full total with the "Airbnb payout" method, dated that day, so the booking is fully paid at once (payment status stays derived, per ADR 0001). We rejected storing the guest price because the resort never receives Airbnb's fee, so every Airbnb booking would show a balance that is never paid. We rejected recording the payout when it reaches the bank because Airbnb has already taken the guest's money and the family does not want to chase it. When a manager changes an Airbnb booking's total, including after a cancellation with a partial payout, the automatic payment follows it; a payout of zero soft-deletes that payment. When staff change a booking's source to Airbnb, the app creates that payment for the current total, or reactivates a soft-deleted one; when they change it away from Airbnb, it soft-deletes it. The guest price and Airbnb's fee are not stored; if analytics need the fee cost later, they are new fields, not a change to what the total means.
