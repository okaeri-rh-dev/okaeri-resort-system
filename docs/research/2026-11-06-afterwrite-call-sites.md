## Decisions this raises (for grilling #344)

1. **Rebase the plan on main or rewrite it?** Recommended: amend in place. Over 95% of sites are same-hook, line-shifted; the changes are a handful of table lists plus one gone hook.
2. **`BOOKING_CREATE_WRITES` gains `payments`** (Airbnb payout insert) **and `audit_logs`?** Recommended: add `payments`; leave `audit_logs` to the module (it adds it always).
3. **`useUpdateBooking` reports `("bookings", "guests", "booking_units", "payments")`.** Recommended: yes, since `set_booking_total` writes the last two whenever a total is passed.
4. **Guest retention mutations (`useAnonymizeGuest`, `useDeleteGuest`) get a route constant** (e.g. `GUEST_RETENTION_WRITES = ["guests","guest_assignments","bookings","booking_units","payments","audit_logs_archive"]`) next to `callGuestRetention`. Recommended: yes, one constant for both routes; `delete_guest` writes a superset only by deleting rows.
5. **`availability_blackouts` READERS = `[blackouts root, ...AVAILABILITY, availability-calendar root, unitKeys.all]`, and Task 1 keeps `availabilityKeys.blackouts` and `.calendar`.** Recommended: yes; `booking_unit_availability` READERS also add the `availability-calendar` root.
6. **Add `contact_channels: [contactChannelKeys.all]`** and the four `useContactChannels` mutations to Task 5. Recommended: yes.
7. **Block hooks (`useCreateBlocks`, `useUpdateBlock`, `useDeleteBlock`) call `afterWrite("availability_blackouts")`; `useCancelBookingsAndSaveBlock` calls `afterWrite("bookings", "availability_blackouts")` only on `status === "saved"`.** That breaks the plan's "always `onSettled`" rule, since a `conflicts_changed` result wrote nothing. Recommended: keep the call in `onSuccess` behind the saved check, and write the exception into Global Constraints.
8. **Dashboard refresh: `refetch()` from `useAllBookings` or keep invalidating units too?** The pane now shows cabins from `useBlockCabins`. Recommended: refetch bookings and units (two `refetch()` calls), not one; or exempt Dashboard from the lint rule.
9. **Drop the `useBatchUpdateUnitsStatus` row** from Task 5. Recommended: yes (gone).
10. **`ENTITY_TABLES` typed on `EditableEntityType`** (unit, guest, expense). Recommended: yes; the plan's `Record<EntityType, …>` would not type-check against today's hook without dead entries.
